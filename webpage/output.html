Frequently asked questions

What is JSON-LD?
JSON-LD (JavaScript Object Notation for Linked Data) is a way to add structured data to your web pages. Search engines like Google use it to understand your content and display rich results in search.
Do I need coding skills?
No! That's the whole point. Our visual editor lets you build JSON-LD using drag-and-drop nodes and forms — no coding required.
Can I use this with any website?
Yes! JSON-LD is universal. Export your structured data and embed it in any website — WordPress, Shopify, custom HTML, or any framework.
Is my data safe?
Absolutely. Your projects are encrypted and stored securely. We never share or sell your data. You can export and back up your work anytime.
Do you offer a free plan?
Yes! Our free plan includes 3 active projects and basic templates — perfect for personal projects and learning.
